# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers of **A.B.I.R | Beyond Ordinary** pledge to make participation in this project a welcoming, respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free experience for everyone.

We are committed to creating an environment where people can contribute ideas, report issues, suggest improvements, ask questions, and collaborate regardless of experience level, background, identity, or skill.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include:

* Being respectful and considerate of others.
* Communicating constructively and professionally.
* Welcoming different opinions and perspectives.
* Giving and receiving constructive feedback.
* Focusing criticism on ideas, code, designs, or contributions rather than individuals.
* Helping newcomers understand the project and its contribution process.
* Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

* Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or personal attacks.
* Trolling, insulting, or deliberately provoking other contributors.
* Publishing or sharing someone's private information without permission.
* Sexualized language, unwanted advances, or inappropriate content.
* Threats of violence or encouraging harm.
* Deliberately disrupting discussions, contributions, or project activities.
* Any other behavior that would reasonably be considered inappropriate or unacceptable in a professional open-source community.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including:

* GitHub issues and pull requests.
* Discussions and comments.
* Project-related communication channels.
* Any other environment where someone is representing or participating in the **A.B.I.R | Beyond Ordinary** project.

Project maintainers may also apply these standards when behavior occurring outside official project spaces has a significant impact on the safety or well-being of the project community.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing the standards of acceptable behavior.

They may take any action they consider appropriate in response to unacceptable behavior, including:

* Removing inappropriate comments, issues, pull requests, or other contributions.
* Issuing a warning.
* Temporarily restricting participation.
* Permanently banning someone from project spaces.
* Reporting serious or illegal conduct to the appropriate authorities when necessary.

Enforcement decisions will be made fairly and proportionately to the situation.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it privately to the project maintainer through the contact method provided in the repository.

Please include as much relevant information as possible, such as:

* What happened.
* When and where it happened.
* Who was involved.
* Any relevant links, screenshots, or other evidence.

Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible.

Please do not use public GitHub issues to report harassment, threats, security-sensitive information, or other private matters.

## No Retaliation

Retaliation against anyone who makes a good-faith report or participates in an investigation is not acceptable.

Reports should be made honestly and responsibly. Knowingly submitting false accusations or deliberately abusing the reporting process may itself be considered a violation of this Code of Conduct.

## Community Expectations

A.B.I.R | Beyond Ordinary is built around learning, experimentation, creativity, and collaboration.

You do not need to be an expert to contribute. Questions are welcome, mistakes are part of learning, and constructive discussion is encouraged.

The goal is not simply to write better code, but to build a community where people can **create, learn, improve, and go beyond ordinary** together.
